🐸 BrainFrog Voice …

BrainFrog reads your live Twitch chat out loud on request -- and can just chat with you too -- in a friendly voice of your choosing. Runs as a plain background app -- no VR session, no game hook, so it keeps talking no matter what you're playing. Keys are saved on this PC only.

Let BrainFrog read my live chat
Read-only and anonymous: no login or token. Messages that moderators delete are removed, so he can't read them.
Skip any message or username containing a slur (recommended). Handles symbols, spacing and look-alike letters.
Also read automatically whenever new messages arrive
Off by default: reading on every single new message can talk over your game at a bad moment. With this off, BrainFrog only reads when you say the wake word below (or ask "what's chat saying").

Wake word

Listen on the mic
Runs locally on this PC (no audio leaves your machine). Three things it understands: ask what chat is saying (reads what's new, nothing repeated) — say the wake word plus a question (an actual answer, in character) — or just say the wake word to start chatting. Try saying "brainfrog", "what's chat saying?", or "brainfrog, how's it going?" once it says it's listening below.
